Questions

In short.

What’s included?

Your named team, on call all year. Leading private hospitals, regenerative medicine done properly, your records kept ready, every visit coordinated, a white-glove arrival, and the finest tables, suites and resorts, arranged for you. For a household of up to four.

What do the tiers mean?

Each membership adds a country. USD 35,000 is Malaysia, USD 50,000 adds Thailand, and USD 65,000 adds Korea. A household of up to four, all year.

What regenerative care can you arrange?

Approved treatments at accredited hospitals in Malaysia, Thailand and Korea, including registered clinical programmes. Your specialists advise; the decision is yours. More.

What else can you arrange?

The hardest tables, the finest suites and resorts, private experiences, and beautiful places to recover. Booked in your name, at the supplier’s price. More on access.

What’s extra?

Hospital bills, paid directly to the hospital. Aviation, chauffeurs, hotels and dining, booked in your name and billed to you at the supplier’s price.

How is my care kept private?

Discretion by default. Records remain with your doctors and your team, and are shared only when you wish it. More on privacy.

How do I join?

By introduction, for a limited number of families. Request one, and we will reply in person.
